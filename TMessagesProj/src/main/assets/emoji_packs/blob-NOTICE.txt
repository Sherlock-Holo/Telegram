Bundled emoji pack "Blob" (emoji_packs/blob.zip)
================================================

The images in blob.zip are derived from Blobmoji (https://github.com/C1710/blobmoji),
which is based on Noto Emoji (https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji),
Copyright Google Inc. and the respective creators; see the Blobmoji AUTHORS_Noto,
CONTRIBUTORS_Noto and CONTRIBUTORS_Blob.md files. Noto is a trademark of Google Inc.

License of the images: Apache License, Version 2.0
(full text: blob-LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt next to this file). Flag images under
Blobmoji's third_party/region-flags are in the public domain or otherwise exempt
from copyright.

Modifications made for this app (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)): the PNG exports of the
Blobmoji releases (png-15.0.zip and png-15.1.zip) were cropped to remove the side
bearing, downscaled to 64x64 px, and renamed to the lower-case Unicode code point
sequence of the emoji (for example 1f600.png, 1f468-200d-1f469-200d-1f467.png).
3569 images; a few of the newest emoji have no Blob image and fall back to the
built-in emoji. The pack is installed by EmojiPackManager.installBundled().

The Blobmoji/Noto images are NOT covered by the GPL of the app's source code.
