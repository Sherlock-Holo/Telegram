## Blob emoji pack (bundled)

`TMessagesProj/src/main/assets/emoji_packs/blob.zip` contains emoji images derived from
[Blobmoji](https://github.com/C1710/blobmoji) (based on
[Noto Emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji), Copyright Google Inc. and the respective creators),
licensed under the Apache License 2.0. The images were cropped, downscaled to 64x64 px and renamed to
code point file names. See `TMessagesProj/src/main/assets/emoji_packs/blob-NOTICE.txt` and
`blob-LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt` in the same directory (they are also shipped inside the APK assets).
